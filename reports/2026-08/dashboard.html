Hypotheses

  • The primary driver of margin compression is rising hosting/infrastructure unit costs outpacing revenue growth (COGS +27% vs revenue +3% over Feb-Aug) — this looks like an infrastructure scaling/efficiency problem rather than a one-off, and warrants vendor/usage review.
  • August's marketing overspend (+28% vs budget) appears to be a one-time or discretionary spend rather than a structural shift, since prior months (Jun/Jul) did not show this pattern — but this is unconfirmed and should be validated with whoever approved the marketing spend.
  • Payroll growth is currently well-managed and not the primary risk to runway; the CS and Sales overtime/budget overages are modest and likely workload-driven rather than a control issue.
  • At current trend (no intervention), the company is on a trajectory toward faster cash depletion than the current 4.4-month runway estimate implies, since burn has been accelerating (not flat) for three consecutive months.

Recommendations

  • Prioritize a hosting/infrastructure cost review (vendor contracts, usage efficiency, architecture) given COGS growth is outpacing revenue by a wide margin and is the largest identified lever on gross margin.
  • Have FP&A and the budget owner review and confirm whether the August marketing overspend (+$18.2K vs budget) was approved/one-off or is likely to recur in September.
  • Given 4.4 months of runway and an accelerating (not stabilizing) burn trend with no cost-reduction plan on file, management/board should discuss near-term financing options and/or a formal burn-reduction plan before runway compresses further.
  • Ask FP&A to build an explicit cost-reduction scenario (not just revenue-growth extrapolation) into the forecast model so runway projections reflect planned mitigations, not just trailing trend.
  • No action needed on the $0 balance-sheet liabilities — controller confirmed this is expected under pay-as-incurred AP handling, not an error.

Open questions

  • What specifically drove the 27% rise in hosting/infrastructure costs since May (usage growth, price increases, new services, inefficiency)? No specialist was asked to break this down at the vendor/usage level — would need AP or a dedicated infra cost review.
  • Was the August marketing overspend a planned one-time campaign or an emerging trend? FP&A flagged it as new but did not confirm the underlying cause or leadership's intent for September.
  • Is there a company-approved plan (financing, cost cuts, revenue acceleration) to address the sub-5-month runway, or is management currently unaware of the accelerating burn trend FP&A identified?
  • The company uses pay-as-incurred AP with no liability accrual — is this the intended accounting policy going forward, or should period-end AP accruals be introduced for more accurate point-in-time liability reporting?
