Hypotheses

  • The primary driver of the widening operating loss is COGS (hosting/infrastructure) growth outpacing revenue growth, not payroll or opex overspend — payroll and most departmental salary lines are actually running at or under budget.
  • The flat 4.4-month runway figure likely understates urgency: if hosting COGS growth and the recent loss-acceleration trend continue unchecked, FP&A's trend-based projection implies cash could be exhausted around November-December 2026, sooner than the flat-burn estimate suggests.
  • The Marketing overage (+28% vs budget) is a secondary, more controllable contributor to the loss compared to the structural COGS issue.
  • A $0.00 total-liabilities balance is plausible in a company that pays AP down to zero and may not carry material accrued liabilities, but it's atypical enough (no accrued payroll, payroll taxes payable, or deferred revenue) to warrant independent confirmation rather than being accepted at face value.
